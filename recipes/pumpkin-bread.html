Recipes: Pumpkin Bread

Description

This pumpkin bread is moist and full of warm spices. This bread tastes even better the next day, so great for the holidays!

Ingredients

  • 1 15 oz. can pumpkin puree
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • 2/3 cup water
  • 3 cups white sugar
  • 3 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1/2 tsp ground cloves
  • 1/4 tsp ground ginger

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees. Grease and flour 2 9x5 loaf pans.
  2. Mix all the dry ingredients together.
  3. Mix all the lquid ingredients together in a different bowl, make sure to blend well.
  4. Mix all the ingredients together until it's just blended.
  5. Pour into prepared pans. And bake for about 50 mins, you will know it's done when a toothpick comes out clean from the middle.

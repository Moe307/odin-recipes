Recipes: Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish that is hearty, flavorful, and great for the whole family.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground Beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella Chesse
  • Ricotta Chesse

Steps

  1. Cook the noodles
  2. Brown and drain the ground beef
  3. Mix the sauce into the meat
  4. Layer the noodles, then sauce, and chesse. Repeat until pan is full.
  5. Bake at 375 for 45 mins

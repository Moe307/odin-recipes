Recipes: Easy Homemade Pizza Dough

Description

This is a fast and easy pizza dough recipe when you don't have time to wait for it to rise. You just mix and give it 5 mins to rest and you are ready to make pizza.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of warm water
  • 1 (.25 oz) package active dry yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 1/2 cups bread flour (a must)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt

Steps

  1. Add the sugar to the warm water. Mix, let sit for 10 mins (the yeast needs to bloom)
  2. Have flour and salt mixed, add the water and oil and kned until smooth
  3. Let rest for 5 mins
  4. Now the fun! The getting it into the shape you want. Be sure to do a lighty floured surface when you are rolling the dough.
  5. Top with what you like. Bake at 400 degrees for 15 to 20 mins until golden brown.
